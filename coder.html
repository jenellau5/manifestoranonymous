How the guided version works

Experience → feeling → evidence → center → circuit → gate → build

Nothing is guessed for you. The coder only removes gates that do not belong to the center and circuit you chose, so you compare a short list instead of all 64.

01 · 02Experience + feeling

Listen all the way through, then name your reaction in plain words.

03Evidence

Point to the lyric, vocal shift, beat, repetition or silence that carried it.

04 · 05Center + circuit

Locate the kind of energy, then how it behaves: individual, collective or tribal.

06 · 07Gate + build

Compare the narrowed gates, record why, and save the entry to your private pattern log.

A song can carry more than one gate, and your reading can change with a transit or an emotional wave. That is pattern recognition, not a wrong answer.
