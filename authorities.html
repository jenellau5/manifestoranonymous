FIND YOURS

Your chart provider should label Authority directly.

If your Solar Plexus is defined, you have Emotional Authority. Manifestors without defined Solar Plexus may have Splenic or Ego Authority depending on the chart's definition. Do not try to choose the one you like best.

EMOTIONAL MANIFESTOR

Urgency is not clarity.

Excitement can make a yes feel absolute. A low can make the same thing look terrible. Your job is not to become neutral; it is to stop giving one emotional point permanent authority over a significant decision.

Watch for

Informing everyone at the emotional peak and then feeling trapped by your own announcement. You can update an inform when clarity changes.

SPLENIC MANIFESTOR

The first hit may be quieter than the fear that follows it.

Splenic awareness is immediate and present-focused. It can arrive as tightening, release, alertness, ease, a body no, or a knowing that does not come with a PowerPoint.

Watch for

Demanding a courtroom-quality explanation from a body signal that was never trying to write a thesis.

EGO MANIFESTOR

Capability is not consent.

Ego Authority is tied to will, desire and what you genuinely want to commit your willpower toward.

Watch for

Saying yes because your competence was questioned. “I can” and “I want to” are not synonyms.
