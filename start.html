TYPE + AURA

A motor connected to the Throat; no defined Sacral.

A Manifestor is built to initiate and create impact through a closed, repelling aura. That aura protects autonomy; it is not proof that people dislike you. The undefined Sacral means work arrives in bursts rather than a dependable all-day supply.

STRATEGY

Inform before the move.

Inform the people who will actually be affected. It is not asking permission, explaining until they agree or announcing every private thought. A clean inform reduces avoidable resistance while leaving your initiation yours.

AUTHORITY

Let the body decide.

Emotional Authority waits for clarity across the wave. Splenic Authority notices the quiet, immediate instinct. Ego Authority listens for genuine desire and the will to commit. Urgency, anger and mental certainty are not substitutes.

ANGER + PEACE

Use the signal; do not obey it blindly.

Anger can flag control, interruption, overwork, an unspoken boundary or movement against Authority. Peace often feels like less friction around being yourself—not permanent calm and not compliance.

CENTERS

Defined is consistent; undefined is receptive.

Defined centers provide reliable mechanics. Undefined and open centers amplify and learn from what moves through them. Neither is better. For a Manifestor the Sacral is undefined, while a motor must connect to the defined Throat.

GATES + CHANNELS

Potentials become circuitry.

A Gate is one specific potential in a center. Two activated Gates across a pathway define a Channel and connect centers. A hanging Gate can become louder around a person or transit that supplies its other side.

PROFILE

The role through which the design is lived.

Your two lines describe conscious and unconscious learning patterns—not a personality box. Notice projection, experimentation, networks, natural talent and life stage without using Profile to overrule Authority.

RED + BLACK

Design body; Personality awareness.

Black activations tend to be consciously recognizable. Red activations may be obvious to other people before they are obvious to you. A placement can be both. Neither side is more authentic.

VARIABLES

Conditions, not commandments.

Determination, Environment, Perspective and Motivation refine how the body takes in, where it functions, what awareness notices and how the mind engages. They support the experiment; they do not tell a Manifestor when to initiate.

THE MANIFESTOR THREAD: Run every chart detail through Authority, informing, impact, boundaries, anger/peace and honest non-Sacral recovery. No isolated Gate, Profile or arrow outranks the whole design.
