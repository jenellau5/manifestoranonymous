MANIFESTOR BASICS

Start with what changes your real life.

Your chart may contain hundreds of details. These five mechanics are the useful starting point.

AURA

Closed and repelling does not mean unlikeable.

Your aura can feel private, protective and difficult for other people to read. When they cannot tell what you are about to do, they may fill in the blanks themselves.

IN REAL LIFE

Someone assumes you are angry or secretive when you were simply thinking, working or preparing to move.

Notice: Where are you exhausting yourself trying to prove that you are safe, nice or understandable?

INITIATION

Moving first does not mean moving impulsively.

You do not need a Sacral response or invitation before beginning something. Your Authority still determines whether the movement is correct for you. Every exciting idea does not need to become an initiation.

IN REAL LIFE

You have a new idea. It may be yours to initiate, but that does not mean you must announce it, build it and sell it tonight.

Notice: Does the movement still feel correct after urgency, pressure and proving are removed?

INFORMING

Informing is not asking for permission.

Tell the people directly affected what you are doing so they have enough information to adjust. You do not need to explain every motive or turn your decision into a vote.

IN REAL LIFE

“I’m leaving at 4:00. If you are coming with me, be ready by then.”

Notice: Are you informing clearly, asking for permission or explaining until everyone approves?

ENERGY + REST

Powerful energy is not endless energy.

Manifestor energy often moves in bursts followed by recovery. A productive day does not prove that you should maintain the same pace forever. Rest belongs inside the cycle.

IN REAL LIFE

You initiate, build intensely and then need space. Recovery does not automatically mean the initiation was wrong.

Notice: Are you still working because energy is available, or because stopping makes you feel guilty?

ANGER + PEACE

Anger is information. It is not a court order.

Anger can point toward control, interruption, overwork, ignored boundaries, suppressed informing or movement against your Authority. It deserves attention, but every angry conclusion is not automatically correct.

IN REAL LIFE

You are furious that someone interrupted your plan. You may need to inform, hold a boundary, change direction or release the reaction.

Notice: What is the anger protecting, and what still feels correct after the charge settles?

YOUR AUTHORITY

Your mind is brilliant. It still does not get the final vote.

Your chart will name one of these three Authorities. Read yours rather than choosing the one you like best.

EMOTIONAL

Clarity over time.

A defined Solar Plexus means significant decisions need time. Excitement can make a yes feel absolute. A low can make the same thing look terrible. Let the emotional intensity move before locking in.

Watch for: Informing everyone at the emotional peak and then feeling trapped by your own announcement.

SPLENIC

Immediate body knowing.

Splenic awareness is quiet, immediate and focused on the present. It may arrive as tightening, release, alertness, ease or a knowing that disappears when analysis begins.

Watch for: Demanding a perfect explanation from a body signal that was never trying to write a thesis.

EGO

Desire and will.

Ego Authority asks what you genuinely want and where you want to spend finite willpower. Capability alone does not create a correct commitment.

Watch for: Saying yes because your competence was questioned. “I can” and “I want to” are not the same.

YOUR PROFILE

Your two numbers describe how your life process works.

Choose the Profile listed on your chart. The first number is conscious. The second can be more visible to other people before you recognize it yourself.
