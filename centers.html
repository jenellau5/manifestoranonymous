CENTERS

Nine centers. One of yours has to stay white.

Centers tell you where energy is defined and consistent versus where you are more open to conditioning and amplification.

01

Head

Inspiration + mental pressure

Pressure to think, question and be inspired.

Manifestor lens: Undefined Head pressure can make other people’s questions feel urgent. Inspiration is not automatically an initiation.
02

Ajna

Concepts + mental processing

How the mind conceptualizes, organizes and holds information.

Manifestor lens: A defined or undefined Ajna still does not become your Authority. Your mind can explain a decision without making it.
03

Throat

Expression + manifestation

Communication, action and bringing energy into expression.

Manifestor lens: A Manifestor has a defined Throat connected to a motor. Your impact often becomes obvious here, but you are not required to speak constantly.
04

G Center

Identity + direction + love

Sense of self, direction and love themes.

Manifestor lens: Your direction can be consistent or variable depending on definition. Being a Manifestor does not require a fixed identity performance.
05

Ego/Heart

Will + value + resources

Willpower, promises, material value and self-worth themes.

Manifestor lens: If Ego Authority is yours, desire and will become central to decisions. Otherwise, willpower still does not replace Authority.
06

Spleen

Instinct + health + survival

Present-moment instinct, immune awareness and fear themes.

Manifestor lens: Splenic Manifestors may have Authority here. If you do not, splenic hits can still be useful information without automatically becoming the final decision.
07

Solar Plexus

Emotion + desire + sensitivity

Emotional waves, feelings, desire and relational chemistry.

Manifestor lens: If this center is defined, you are an Emotional Manifestor and significant decisions need time rather than forced certainty.
08

Sacral

Life force + work + sexuality

Sustainable generative life-force and response energy.

Manifestor lens: YOURS MUST BE UNDEFINED. This is one of the central mechanics that separates a Manifestor from a Generator or Manifesting Generator.
09

Root

Pressure + stress + drive

Adrenal pressure to move, begin and get things done.

Manifestor lens: Root pressure can feel like “do it now.” Pressure is not Authority, and finishing quickly just to escape pressure can create unnecessary initiations.
THE MANIFESTOR NON-NEGOTIABLE: your Sacral Center cannot be defined. If it is colored in on the chart you are using, re-check the chart or Type.
