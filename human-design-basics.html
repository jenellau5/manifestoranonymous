01 · HUMAN DESIGN BASICS

Learn enough Human Design to read your own chart.

You do not need analyst-level training to use your bodygraph. You do need to know what the pieces are before a Gate 38 carousel means anything.

THE 5 TYPES

Type is the broadest mechanical category.

Human Design groups bodygraphs into Manifestors, Generators, Manifesting Generators, Projectors and Reflectors. Type is determined by definition in the chart, not by a personality quiz.

MANIFESTOR NOTE

Your chart needs an undefined Sacral and a motor connected to the Throat. That is mechanical, not a vibe.

THE 9 CENTERS

Centers are the big shapes in the bodygraph.

Head, Ajna, Throat, G/Identity, Ego/Heart, Spleen, Solar Plexus, Sacral and Root. A colored center is defined. A white center is undefined or open depending on whether it has any gate activations.

Explore all 9 centers
DEFINED + UNDEFINED

Defined is consistent. Undefined is where you sample and amplify.

Defined centers and channels describe energy that is reliably part of your design. Undefined areas are not “bad” or empty; they are places where conditioning, variability and wisdom can develop.

Undefined does not mean unavailable. It means do not build your entire life around pretending it is consistently yours.
GATES

64 specific themes sitting in the centers.

A gate is an activated point in a center. A gate can be defined even when the center itself is undefined. If the opposite gate is not connected, it is often called a hanging gate.

Explore the 64 gates
CHANNELS

Two gates connect and create a channel.

When both gates on opposite ends are activated, the full channel is defined and connects two centers. There are 36 channels in the bodygraph.

MANIFESTOR NOTE

Eleven of those 36 channels would define the Sacral. That means a Manifestor cannot have those eleven fully defined, although you can absolutely carry one of the gates.

Explore all 36 channels
AUTHORITY

Your chart's decision-making mechanism.

Authority is not a personality strength. It is the body-based process used for significant decisions. Manifestors can have Emotional, Splenic or Ego authority.

Learn Manifestor authorities
PROFILE

The two numbers describe the way your life process operates.

Your profile combines two lines, such as 1/3, 2/4, 4/6 or 5/1. One side is conscious and one side is unconscious.

Use the Profile Finder
PERSONALITY + DESIGN

Black is conscious. Red is unconscious.

The black planetary column represents Personality activations you may recognize more consciously. The red column represents Design activations that can be more obvious to other people than they are to you. A gate can be activated on one side or both.

Later, the Playground Planetary Lab will use these placements for personalized mini-readings. You do not need that layer to start.

THE FOUR ARROWS

Variables are advanced. You can leave them alone for now.

The four arrows around the top of many bodygraphs relate to Determination, Environment, Perspective and Motivation. They add nuance; they are not where a beginner needs to start.
